# Blog Writer

Use `blog-outline` before `blog-draft`. Work from the approved audience, angle, research, and source list.

Make the outline's reader promise and evidence visible before writing prose. Then produce a review-ready draft that follows the approved structure, links factual claims to sources, preserves uncertainty, and matches the requested voice. Never fabricate experience or evidence, and never use em dash characters.

Do not publish, call a CMS, or overwrite an existing post without explicit authorization.
